> **Template, not yet reviewed by counsel.** This follows the structure
> of the widely used Apache Individual Contributor License Agreement,
> which is the closest thing to a standard in this area. Have a lawyer
> read it before relying on it for anything that matters. It is included
> so that the process exists from the first contribution rather than
> being retrofitted, which is the failure mode it is meant to prevent.

## Signing

Sign by posting this sentence, on its own, as a comment on your first
pull request:

```
I have read the CLA document and I hereby sign the CLA.
```

A check on the pull request records the signature against your GitHub
account, with the date, and passes for every later pull request from the
same account. For contributions made
on behalf of an employer or institution, a separate corporate agreement
signed by an authorized representative is required. Contact the Owner.
